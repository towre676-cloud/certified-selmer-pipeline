## Mathematical scope and remaining work

| Layer | Established scope | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Conventional written argument | Rank two, finite 2-Selmer dimension two, 2-primary Sha vanishing; separate height/saturation and analytic arguments | Uses standard mathematical theorems outside Lean |
| Dependency-free Lean core | 42 audited finite declarations with empty transitive axiom lists | No formal rank, Selmer completeness or Sha theorem |
| Optional pinned Mathlib project | Seven audited declarations about the actual rational curve, including absence of nonidentity two-power torsion | Standard Lean axioms; not a Sha or rank theorem |
| Pinned PassageMath/PARI/Arb | Replayed arithmetic, numerical enclosures and four odd-primary upper bounds | Trusted implementations and mathematical hypotheses; not independent Lean certificates |
| Historical receipt hashes | Limited decoded-payload consistency | Neither provenance authentication nor arithmetic truth; exact decimal spelling is not bound |

The portable descent alone proves only an odd-index subgroup generated by P,Q;
the full-basis claim uses the separate height/saturation argument. The infinite
2-Selmer group is `(Q_2/Z_2)^2`, not zero. No end-to-end formal rank/Selmer/Sha
proof, full Sha finiteness or triviality, or full BSD theorem follows. The
3-primary case and a uniform treatment of all remaining primes are open here.
The number-field/maximal-order, ideal/class-group, local-field and Kummer
exact-sequence formal bridges remain substantial work. The generic arithmetic
generator must continue to refuse unsupported certification.
